Motivations

Impact Framework (IF) aims to facilitate the calculation and sharing of environmental impacts of software.

What does the framework promise?

Transform your observations into impact units.

Explore simulation scenarios.

Store your manifests and democratize them.

Measuring the impact of software on parameters such as carbon, water, and energy is complex and nuanced.

Modern applications are composed of many smaller software elements (components) running on different environments, for example, private cloud, public cloud, bare-metal, virtualized, containerized, mobile, laptops, desktops, embedded and IoT.

Transform observations into impacts: Easily observable measurements such as CPU usage, page views, and installations are converted into environmental impacts such as carbon emissions, water usage, energy consumption, and air quality.

Explore simulation scenarios: Discover the environmental impact of changes to your software in real time by examining how your software's environmental performance evolves if your application is moved to the cloud or if its runtime is modified.

Store: Record your observations, chosen plugins, configurations, and calculated environmental impacts in a manifest file. Open the door for others to understand, verify, and challenge the entire process. Allow others to rerun your manifest file, validate your results, or challenge your assumptions. They can modify configurations, select different plugins, and perform the analysis themselves. ---
